[h1]Planet States — Stellaris 4.4[/h1]

Independent, sovereign planets you can trade with, sign a commercial pact with, or conquer.

Four Planet-States spawn at the start of a new game:

[list]
[*] Fanatic Egalitarian — ecumenopolis, sells Consumer Goods
[*] Fanatic Authoritarian — tomb-world caste society, sells Minerals
[*] Fanatic Militarist — organics fight, robots work, sells Alloys
[*] Fanatic Pacifist — gaia world, sells Food
[/list]

Monthly deals: 10 / 20 / 30 / 40 / 50. At 50 trust you can sign a Commercial Pact (+5% Trade).

You can build a starbase in their system. You can only attack them if their capital is inside your borders. They are guarded by a Planet-State Station.

[h1]Compatibility[/h1]

Overwrites the game rule can_take_hostile_actions. Compatible with Merger of Rules. Does not overwrite frontend.gui.

Supported version: 4.4.* (verified 4.4.6).
